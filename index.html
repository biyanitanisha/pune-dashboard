1 · The data

Where the data comes from

Everything on this page was built from open geospatial data for the Katraj area of Pune. The layers were cleaned and analysed in a PostGIS database, published through GeoServer, designed in QGIS and brought to the web in this dashboard.

OpenStreetMap

Roads, buildings, points of interest, land use, green spaces and water, downloaded through Geofabrik.

Ward boundaries

The 15 administrative wards of Pune; Admin Ward 14 Dhankawadi holds most of the study area.

Bus stops

A public-transport stop list (GTFS format) with stop names and codes, 148 stops in the area.

3 · The numbers

What the analysis shows

Summaries from the PostGIS queries: length of roads, distance of bus stops from Katraj Lake and the mix of amenities.

Road length by class (km)

Bus stops by distance from the lake

Amenities by type

  • Residential streets total about 220 km, roughly 70% of the 316.7 km road network, while trunk roads add 19 km.
  • 102 of the 148 bus stops (69%) lie within 1 km of Katraj Lake, and 54 of them within 500 m.
  • The area holds 22 hospitals, 9 clinics and 8 pharmacies, against 53.6 ha of mapped green space and an 18.8 ha lake.
